Trend lifecycle / Timing

Virality is a stage, not a starting point.

The strongest opportunity often appears while a format is still building momentum—before broad visibility brings saturation.

Highest-value window
  1. EmergingEarly repeats
  2. GrowingMomentum builds
  3. ExplodingRapid spread
  4. SaturatedBroad adoption
  5. DecliningAttention fades

The product is being designed to make timing legible—not simply to catalogue what is already viral.
